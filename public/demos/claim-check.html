      Run this activity

      Presenter · 4 minutes

      1. Read claim 2 and find source B together. Explain why the claim is contradicted.
      2. Check claim 5. A session record does not establish who attended.
      3. Ask for a hands-up vote on claim 4, then reveal the prepared check.

      Pairs · 15 minutes

      1. Pick a claim and find the source that speaks to it.
      2. Choose a bin. Write one sentence on paper naming your source and reason.
      3. Compare with a neighbor. Name the extra evidence needed to settle missing claims.

      No devices: Print or read the notice and six cards. Write S, M or C beside each claim with a source letter. Vote by hands.

      Debrief: Which claims were hardest to place? When is “no evidence” the honest answer? What should the library add to its notice?

      Check your reasoning: Name one claim with missing evidence and one contradicted by a source. Explain the difference.

      Prompt to reuse

      For each claim, find the sources that speak to it. Decide: Supported, Missing evidence (the available sources do not establish the claim), or Contradicted (a source says something different). Write the reason in one sentence naming the source.
