Reveal the prepared analysis

Clothing: 6 of 7 fixed. Bikes: 6 of 8. Small electricals: 5 of 9. That is 17 of 24 visits overall.

With four volunteers, the average wait was 12 minutes across 16 visits. With two, it was 25 minutes across 8 visits. But 5 of those 8 visits involved electrical items, compared with 4 of 16 in the larger team’s sessions.

The data shows an association. It cannot tell us whether team size, item difficulty, demand, or another factor caused the waits. These invented visits are a practice dataset.
