Made-up data only

Do not put real names, contact details, student or family records, donor records, payroll data or attendance sheets into any AI tool. Keep the real field names and use invented values in them.

Before anything leaves the table

The output reviewer checks three things: is it accurate, does it contain anything private, and does it sound like your organization. If the answer to any of them is wrong, they can say 'do not send that'.

For coaches
  • Coach, do not drive. The representative keeps the keyboard and makes every decision.
  • Ask which box is thinnest. It is usually Context. Do not fill it in for them.
  • Ask early whether the project touches real records. If it does, build the same thing with the same fields on made-up data.
  • If someone is hesitant about AI, treat it as a considered position and talk it through. Do not argue them out of it.
  • Push on the 'for whom'. 'The team' is not an answer. A named role and a moment of use is.
  • If the table is stuck between two recipes, choose the smaller one. If they cannot show a working piece in 15 seconds, switch to the knowledge base and call it banking a win.

Keep it with your team

  1. Copy the prompt and paste it into your organization's own AI workspace or Project.
  2. Run it there, then have the output reviewer check accuracy, privacy and voice.
  3. Save the prompt and the reviewed output in your own Project so they stay with your team.
  4. Within 48 hours, try the structure on one real task, following your organization's data rules. Save the prompt to your library.
